## 4. Batch-selection method

The 55 draft registry records were screened against their article files, plan metadata, current publication boundary, and existing public topics. Priority was given to:

1. A clear standalone question or task.
2. Practical value without dependence on a client story.
3. Search intent that can be satisfied without volatile statistics.
4. Low topic overlap with the six published Social Media Strategy articles.
5. Opportunities to form useful cross-article paths.
6. Category diversity.
7. A body that is substantially usable and does not need unnecessary rewriting.

The selected set contains three Paid Media articles, three Ecommerce & CRO articles, two Tracking & Analytics articles, one Creative Strategy article, and one SEO article. The Creative Strategy and SEO selections need more editorial work than the other eight, but they were retained because they add important category breadth and have strong underlying topics. No article based on private-client material, an unsupported case study, or an outdated statistical claim was selected.
