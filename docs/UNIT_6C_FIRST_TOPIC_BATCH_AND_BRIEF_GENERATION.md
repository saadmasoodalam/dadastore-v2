# Unit 6C — First Controlled Topic Batch and Brief Generation

## 1. Title

Unit 6C — Seed First Controlled Topic Batch and Generate Article Briefs.

## 2. Status

Existing-library gap review, queue seeding, duplicate protection, deterministic brief generation, validation, and documentation. No article copy or public asset was generated.

## 3. Purpose

Prove the controlled Auto Blogger topic-to-brief path with one bounded five-topic batch while preserving every production publication boundary.

## 4. Existing-library audit

All 61 published registry records and article pages were reviewed through titles, slugs, categories, excerpts, metadata descriptions, H2/H3 headings, inferred intent, article angles, and related-topic opportunities. The library currently emphasizes channel planning, campaign structure, CRO diagnosis, tracking setup, content planning, lifecycle email sequences, and general operating systems.

The audit found missing decision layers around unit economics, marginal budget movement, cross-functional retention operations, post-launch automation governance, and acquisition-channel concentration risk.

## 5. Content-gap methodology

Candidates had to answer a materially different business decision, support 1,400–2,200 useful words, fit a current category, connect to DaDaStore services, support distinct visuals, and have published internal-link paths. Normalized titles, slugs, keywords, excerpts, metadata, headings, inferred intent, category, and proposed visual concepts were compared with the complete library.

## 6. Batch identifier

`batch-001`

Unit 6C extends the validated batch-ID contract to accept controlled sequential IDs such as `batch-001` alongside the dated Unit 6A format.
