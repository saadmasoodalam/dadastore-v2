The previous general framework was replaced with a guided, eight-part operating template. The revised sequence moves from a business objective through audience, platform, content, publishing, engagement, measurement, and review decisions. Each part contains prompts and a concise template output.

The article now varies its pacing through explanatory sections, field lists, output statements, a hypothetical completed example, an operating rhythm, common mistakes, and a final checklist while retaining the approved V3 article shell.

## 11. Template added

The static template covers:

1. Business objective
2. Priority audience and customer problem
3. Platform selection and channel role
4. Content pillars and claim boundaries
5. Publishing approach and team capacity
6. Engagement process and escalation
7. KPI selection and measurement limits
8. Review frequency and next adjustment

Every part includes practical fill-in prompts. The fields cover the objective, audience, problem, chosen platforms, reasons, content pillars, publishing capacity, engagement ownership, KPIs, review frequency, and next adjustment. The template is guidance inside the article; it is not a downloadable product, form, application, or interactive tool.

## 12. Examples added

A hypothetical bookkeeping-firm example demonstrates how the eight decisions connect. It is explicitly presented as fictional and illustrative. It includes no client identity, private proof, performance statistic, or promised outcome.

Short decision examples are also used throughout the template to distinguish operating choices from vague goals.

## 14. Unsupported claims removed or softened

The revision avoids guarantees about growth, reach, sales, revenue, return on ad spend, or platform performance. It does not claim that one strategy, platform mix, cadence, or KPI set fits every small business.

Recommendations are framed as decisions to support with evidence, capacity, audience behavior, and review. Unknown inputs can be recorded as assumptions to test.
