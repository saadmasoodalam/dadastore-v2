## 4. Batch-selection method

The 35 current drafts were screened in five passes:

1. Confirm the registry state, article-folder existence, draft status, null public URL, and absence from the blog home, category page, sitemap, and RSS.
2. Compare each topic with the 26 published articles for search-intent and subject overlap.
3. Inspect metadata, visible reading time, word-count validity, mobile-navigation markup, duplicate IDs, local references, and article structure.
4. Inspect prose for repeated template language and exact duplicated body sentences.
5. Prefer the smallest correction scope while maintaining useful category balance and internal-link opportunities.

The selected batch contains two Social Media Strategy articles, three Paid Media articles, three Ecommerce & CRO articles, and two Tracking & Analytics articles. This is the broadest responsible selection available without knowingly choosing the repeated-template prose found in the later draft groups.

Drafts from Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Marketing Automation, Business Growth Systems, and the remaining unpublished Creative Strategy and SEO groups were not selected. Static review found 16 distinct sentences repeated two or three times inside each screened Batch 5 candidate and 20 such repeated sentence groups inside each screened Batch 6 candidate, with the same boilerplate also repeated across articles. Selecting those drafts would conflict with this unit's explicit instruction to avoid repeated template prose and would create unnecessary major rewrites. Their category-gap value should be reconsidered after a separate template-removal revision unit.
