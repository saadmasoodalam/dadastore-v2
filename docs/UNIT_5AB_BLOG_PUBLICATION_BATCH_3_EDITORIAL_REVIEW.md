## 5. Batch-selection method

Selection used five filters in sequence:

1. **Boundary filter:** the record and article folder had to exist, remain `draft`, retain a null public URL, and remain absent from public listings, sitemap, and RSS.
2. **Editorial-quality filter:** the draft needed a clear standalone question, identifiable audience, practical structure, and a topic-specific visual module.
3. **Library-fit filter:** the topic needed to extend rather than restate the sixteen published articles.
4. **Risk filter:** drafts dependent on volatile instructions, unsupported performance claims, private-client material, or substantial external fact verification were deferred.
5. **Revision-cost filter:** preference went to drafts with sound topic-specific bodies and bounded cleanup needs. Draft families showing materially repetitive scaffolding were deferred rather than selected merely to maximize category variety.

This results in a deliberate four-category batch. Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Marketing Automation, Business Growth Systems, and the remaining Creative Strategy/SEO drafts are not rejected; they are deferred because the strongest current candidates in those groups require more editorial intervention than the ten selected here.
