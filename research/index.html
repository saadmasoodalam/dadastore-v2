Public Research Disclosure Boundary These pages publish only selected public aspects of ongoing independent research. Source code, unpublished implementation details, hardware designs, experimental extensions, and patent-sensitive material remain private unless explicitly released. Deeper technical review is shared selectively according to the reviewer’s field and the question being examined.
For technical reviewers Review requests are especially welcome from people working in FPGA/RTL, embedded systems, formal methods/SMT, protocol design, distributed systems, event sourcing, systems architecture, and multiphysics simulation. Critical counterexamples, equivalent prior art, failure cases, and reproducibility questions are more useful than endorsements.
